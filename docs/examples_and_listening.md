# Free examples and how to listen

## how to listen to a song
Every song in the catalog has a Listen link that opens a YouTube search for the song title and artist. Pick the official upload when you see one. The app does not host or play audio itself. Availability and ads depend on YouTube.

## examples of low versus high energy
Low energy (quiet, slow): Weightless by Marconi Union, Clair de Lune by Claude Debussy, Gymnopedie No. 1 by Erik Satie. High energy (loud, fast): Smells Like Teen Spirit by Nirvana, Master of Puppets by Metallica, Gurenge by LiSA.

## examples of sad versus happy (valence)
Sad sounding: Hallelujah by Jeff Buckley, Mad World by Gary Jules, Moonlight Sonata by Ludwig van Beethoven. Happy sounding: September by Earth, Wind & Fire, Shape of You by Ed Sheeran, Levitating by Dua Lipa.

## examples of acoustic versus electronic
Acoustic (real instruments): Clair de Lune by Claude Debussy, Moonlight Sonata by Ludwig van Beethoven, River Flows in You by Yiruma. Electronic (synthesizers): Blinding Lights by The Weeknd, Strobe by deadmau5, UNDERTALE - CORE (RoBKTA Remix) by RoBKTA.

## examples of slow versus fast tempo
Slow: Clair de Lune by Claude Debussy (60 BPM), Weightless by Marconi Union (60 BPM). Fast: Blinding Lights by The Weeknd (171 BPM), Master of Puppets by Metallica (212 BPM).

## examples with no lyrics (instrumental)
Clair de Lune by Claude Debussy, Cornfield Chase by Hans Zimmer, Megalovania by Toby Fox, Aruarian Dance by Nujabes, Take Five by Dave Brubeck Quartet.

## examples for studying
Clair de Lune by Claude Debussy, Aruarian Dance by Nujabes, Cornfield Chase by Hans Zimmer, Sweden by C418, Undertale - Once Upon A Time by Pennoink.

## examples for working out
Smells Like Teen Spirit by Nirvana, Lose Yourself by Eminem, Gurenge by LiSA, Uptown Funk by Mark Ronson ft. Bruno Mars, Master of Puppets by Metallica.

## examples for falling asleep
Weightless by Marconi Union, Clair de Lune by Claude Debussy, Gymnopedie No. 1 by Erik Satie, River Flows in You by Yiruma.

## examples by genre
Pop: Shape of You by Ed Sheeran. Rock: Dreams by Fleetwood Mac. Hip hop: Lose Yourself by Eminem. Jazz: Take Five by Dave Brubeck Quartet. Classical: Clair de Lune by Claude Debussy. Metal: Master of Puppets by Metallica. Reggae: Three Little Birds by Bob Marley and The Wailers. Country: Take Me Home, Country Roads by John Denver. Latin: Mas Que Nada by Sergio Mendes and Brasil '66. Game music: Megalovania by Toby Fox. Electronic: Strobe by deadmau5.

## examples of well known versus hidden gem songs
Well known (popularity 90 or more): Blinding Lights by The Weeknd, Shape of You by Ed Sheeran, One Summer's Day by Joe Hisaishi. Less known (popularity under 70): Weightless by Marconi Union, Undertale - Ruins by Malcolm Robinson Music, Deltarune - Beginning by Pennoink.

## examples by era
1950s to 1960s: Take Five by Dave Brubeck Quartet, Here Comes the Sun by The Beatles. 1970s: Bohemian Rhapsody by Queen, Hotel California by Eagles. 1990s: Smells Like Teen Spirit by Nirvana. 2010s and 2020s: Blinding Lights by The Weeknd, Sunflower by Post Malone and Swae Lee.

## examples of songs in other languages
Japanese: Gurenge by LiSA, Hotaru no Hikari by Rainych Ran. Portuguese: Mas Que Nada by Sergio Mendes and Brasil '66.
