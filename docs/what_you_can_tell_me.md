# What you can tell the app: the preferences you can give

## what preferences can I tell you about
You can describe your taste in plain words. You do not need numbers. Any two preferences are enough to get recommendations, and you can add more at any time. Here is everything you can give, with an example of each:
- a mood: "calm", "happy", "sad", "chill", "romantic", "nostalgic", "uplifting"
- an occasion, meaning what you will listen for: "studying", "working out", "driving", "going to sleep", "a party", "cooking"
- a genre: "pop", "jazz", "classical", "lofi", "rock", "hiphop", "anime-style j-pop"
- how intense the music is (energy): "quiet and gentle" or "loud and fast"
- how happy or sad it sounds (valence): "sad songs" or "cheerful songs"
- speed (tempo): "slow" or "fast"
- how easy it is to dance to (danceability)
- acoustic or electronic: "acoustic" or "electronic"
- vocals or instrumental: "no lyrics" or "with singing"
- how famous it is (popularity): "well known hits" or "hidden gems"
- an era (decade): "90s music", "2010s"
- song length: "short songs" or "long songs"
- a language: "English", "Japanese"
- explicit songs: "no explicit songs"

## songs you like
You can also name songs, which is often the easiest way to say what you want. Examples: "more songs like Clair de Lune", "I loved Hotaru no Hikari", "I did not like Get Lucky". A song you ask for by name is shown first in your next list, and the rest of the list is similar songs. A song you dislike is left out. A small typo in the title is fine. If the song is not in the catalog, the app says so and uses the same artist's song if there is one.

## only some languages
Say "only English or Japanese songs" to keep the list to those languages. This is a strict filter, not a hint: songs in other languages are removed. Instrumentals have no language, so they are removed too. The catalog has songs in English, Japanese and Portuguese.

## ideas for what to say
Not sure what to say? Try one of these:
- "calm songs for studying"
- "high energy workout music, nothing explicit"
- "happy upbeat songs to sing along to"
- "sad piano music for a rainy night"
- "more songs like Clair de Lune"
- "only English or Japanese songs, something chill"

## how ratings teach the app
After you get a list, type "rate" in the console, or use the rating controls in the web app. Give each song 1 to 10 and, if you like, a short comment such as "too slow". The app learns your taste from all your ratings so far, not just the latest round, and shows a new list of songs you have not heard. Every song has a listen link so you can hear it.

## commands
In the console you can type: recommend (show songs now), rate (rate the songs), profile (show what the app knows), skip (skip a question), refine (answer a few more questions), reset (start over), and quit.
