# Genres, moods, mood tags, and occasions: the words you can use

These four are labels (words), not numbers. The recommender matches them to the words in the catalog, so it helps to know which words exist.

## genre
Genre is the style or category of music. The recommender gives full points for an exact match, partial credit (75 percent) when the labels share text (for example "pop" and "j-pop"), and none otherwise.
Exact genre labels (always use these exact words): alternative, ambient, blues, classical, country, electro, folk, funk, hiphop, indie, j-pop, jazz, latin, lofi, metal, orchestral, pop, reggae, rnb, rock, soundtrack.
Genres in the catalog, with examples:
- pop: Blinding Lights by The Weeknd, Shape of You by Ed Sheeran, Someone Like You by Adele.
- j-pop (Japanese pop): Gurenge by LiSA.
- rock: Bohemian Rhapsody by Queen, Dreams by Fleetwood Mac, Mr. Brightside by The Killers.
- metal: Master of Puppets by Metallica.
- hiphop (rap): Lose Yourself by Eminem, Sunflower by Post Malone and Swae Lee.
- rnb (rhythm and blues): Redbone by Childish Gambino, Pink + White by Frank Ocean.
- funk: Uptown Funk by Mark Ronson ft. Bruno Mars, September by Earth, Wind & Fire.
- jazz: Take Five by Dave Brubeck Quartet, Fly Me to the Moon by Frank Sinatra.
- blues: At Last by Etta James.
- classical: Clair de Lune by Claude Debussy, Moonlight Sonata by Ludwig van Beethoven, River Flows in You by Yiruma.
- orchestral (game and film style arrangements): Undertale - Once Upon A Time by Pennoink, One Summer's Day by Joe Hisaishi.
- soundtrack (film and game scores): Cornfield Chase by Hans Zimmer, Megalovania by Toby Fox, Stardew Valley Overture by ConcernedApe.
- electro (electronic dance music): Strobe by deadmau5, Sunset Lover by Petit Biscuit.
- ambient (slow atmospheric background music): Weightless by Marconi Union, Sweden by C418.
- lofi (mellow, jazzy, study-friendly beats): Aruarian Dance by Nujabes.
- folk: Hallelujah by Jeff Buckley.
- country: Take Me Home, Country Roads by John Denver.
- reggae: Three Little Birds by Bob Marley and The Wailers.
- latin: Mas Que Nada by Sergio Mendes and Brasil '66.
- indie: Mad World by Gary Jules.
- alternative: Feel Good Inc. by Gorillaz.
If a genre is not in this list, the catalog has no songs with that exact label; the closest genre will be suggested instead.

## mood
Mood is the single main feeling of a song. It is an exact match: matching earns full points, a different mood earns none, even a close one (for example "calm" and "relaxed" count as different).
Exact mood labels (always use these exact words): calm, chill, energetic, happy, intense, nostalgic, relaxed, romantic, sad, uplifting.
Moods in the catalog, with examples:
- happy: Levitating by Dua Lipa, Three Little Birds by Bob Marley and The Wailers.
- calm: Clair de Lune by Claude Debussy, Weightless by Marconi Union.
- relaxed: Gymnopedie No. 1 by Erik Satie, Take Five by Dave Brubeck Quartet.
- chill: Sunset Lover by Petit Biscuit, Redbone by Childish Gambino.
- energetic: Blinding Lights by The Weeknd, Mr. Brightside by The Killers.
- intense: Smells Like Teen Spirit by Nirvana, Master of Puppets by Metallica.
- uplifting: Here Comes the Sun by The Beatles, Spring by Antonio Vivaldi.
- romantic: At Last by Etta James, River Flows in You by Yiruma.
- sad: Hallelujah by Jeff Buckley, Moonlight Sonata by Ludwig van Beethoven.
- nostalgic: Hotel California by Eagles.

## mood_tags
Mood tags are extra descriptive words attached to each song, such as "dreamy", "gentle", "powerful", "cozy", "angsty", "motivational", "melancholy", "groovy", "cinematic". They add detail beyond the single mood. The score for tags is the share of your wanted tags that the song has: if you want 2 tags and the song has 1, the song earns half the points.
Examples: Clair de Lune has dreamy, gentle, piano. Master of Puppets has aggressive, heavy, fast. Aruarian Dance has study, mellow, jazzy.

## occasions
Occasions are situations where a song fits. The recommender matches your wanted occasions against each song's occasion tags, and the score is the share of your wanted occasions the song has.
Exact occasion labels (always use these exact words, one label per occasion): cooking, date night, dinner, dramatic listening, driving, focus, gaming, late night, meditation, morning, motivation, night drive, party, reflection, relax, road trip, singalong, sleep, study, summer, travel, venting, wedding, workout.
Occasions in the catalog, with examples:
- study and focus (two labels): Clair de Lune, Cornfield Chase by Hans Zimmer, Aruarian Dance by Nujabes.
- sleep: Weightless by Marconi Union, Clair de Lune by Claude Debussy, Gymnopedie No. 1 by Erik Satie.
- relax: Sunset Lover by Petit Biscuit, Three Little Birds by Bob Marley and The Wailers.
- workout: Smells Like Teen Spirit by Nirvana, Lose Yourself by Eminem, Gurenge by LiSA.
- party: Uptown Funk by Mark Ronson ft. Bruno Mars, Levitating by Dua Lipa.
- driving or road trip: Blinding Lights by The Weeknd, Take Me Home, Country Roads by John Denver.
- gaming: Megalovania by Toby Fox, UNDERTALE - CORE by RoBKTA.
- morning: Here Comes the Sun by The Beatles, September by Earth, Wind & Fire.
- date night or dinner: Fly Me to the Moon by Frank Sinatra, At Last by Etta James.
- late night or reflection: Hallelujah by Jeff Buckley, Mad World by Gary Jules, Moonlight Sonata by Ludwig van Beethoven.
- singalong: Bohemian Rhapsody by Queen, Mr. Brightside by The Killers.
- meditation: Weightless by Marconi Union.
